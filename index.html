READ & LEARN

AI has a physical footprint

A short, source-linked guide to water, electricity, carbon, and what local inference actually changes.

01

Why AI can use water

Data centers can consume water directly for cooling and indirectly through electricity generation. Cooling design, weather, location, grid mix, model size, and prompt length all change the result.

02

Local does not mean zero-impact

NexusEco moves inference to your device and keeps prompts out of a remote chat server. Your laptop still uses electricity, while the model download and device manufacturing also have impacts.

03

Why use a smaller model

A 3B model generally needs less memory and computation than a much larger model. It is useful for everyday writing, code, and math, but it is not automatically more accurate.

04

Land is difficult to count

Data centers, power infrastructure, mineral extraction, and chip production affect land and materials. There is no responsible per-chat land conversion, so NexusEco does not invent one.
